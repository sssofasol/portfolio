SELECTED BUSINESS TRANSFORMATIONS

Leadership decisions that transform businesses

Marketing Investment Management

CHALLENGE

As the international business grew, the existing marketing-investment model could no longer support the speed and quality of decision-making required.

SOLUTION

Shifted governance from approving individual initiatives to a unified marketing-investment management system with shared evaluation and decision criteria.

RESULT

Scalable marketing-investment governance system

Customer Value Management

CHALLENGE

Business growth demanded a more systematic approach to the existing customer base, while the CRM transformation would take almost two years.

SOLUTION

Decoupled the technology and business transformations, establishing a customer-value management system before the new CRM platform launched.

RESULT

+25% growth in CRM-channel revenue

International Product Launches

CHALLENGE

A growing number of international launches required a consistent process without sacrificing local-market agility.

SOLUTION

Built an international go-to-market model with shared standards and governed local adaptation.

RESULT

15–20% faster time to market with consistent delivery standards

Scaling an International Marketing Organisation

CHALLENGE

The centralised management model had stopped scaling and was limiting regional ownership.

SOLUTION

Delegated authority to regions alongside accountability for commercial outcomes through a managed-autonomy model.

RESULT

Scalable international marketing organisation

Marketing Investment Effectiveness

CHALLENGE

A significant share of the budget was being spent to offset organisational constraints rather than generate new demand.

SOLUTION

Removed the underlying drivers of dependency on branded search traffic and redirected investment toward higher-return opportunities.

RESULT

−60% spend on branded paid search

Product Portfolio Strategy

CHALLENGE

The company needed a product strategy that could lower the barrier to entry for new customers while increasing sales of core products.

SOLUTION

Defined the role of a new category within the portfolio and developed a customer-journey-led go-to-market model.

RESULT

>$100K in revenue in the first year after launch

International Brand Management

CHALLENGE

International expansion required a unified brand positioning while retaining local relevance.

SOLUTION

Moved brand management from local interpretations to a unified brand-governance system.

RESULT

Unified system for international brand management

LEADERSHIP APPROACH

Five principles for leadership decisions

01

Start with the business objective, not the marketing brief

I anchor decisions in the company’s commercial objectives rather than in marketing tools.

02

Build solutions designed to scale

I create operating models that enable growth without a proportionate increase in management complexity.

03

Simplify management rather than complicate processes

I establish clear systems for decision-making and accountability.

04

Develop leaders, not team dependency

I build environments where leaders make independent decisions and own the outcome.

05

Measure success through commercial results

I focus on the impact of decisions on business performance, not the number of initiatives delivered.
